Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to VocabSpark: Word Game (“VocabSpark,” “the App,” “we,” “our,” or “us”). VocabSpark: Word Game is an English vocabulary word game in which players use letters to find words, complete levels, and explore word information such as definitions, synonyms, antonyms, and example sentences where available.

This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the App, why it may be processed, and the choices available to you. The information handled may depend on the features and third-party services enabled in the version you use.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account to use its core word-game and vocabulary features. We do not directly ask you to provide details such as your name, phone number, or password to play the game.

Information such as game progress, completed levels, saved words, favourites, or app preferences may be stored locally on your device if those features are available. The App does not send this locally stored information to our own servers unless a future feature clearly states otherwise.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message, and use it to respond to your inquiry.

Technical and Diagnostic Information

Third-party services used by the App may automatically process limited technical information, depending on which services are enabled. This may include:

  • Device model, operating system, and app version
  • Crash reports, diagnostics, and performance information
  • General language or regional settings
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction information, where advertising is enabled
  • Purchase or transaction status handled by the relevant app store, if in-app purchases are offered

The information actually processed depends on the services included in your installed version of the App and your device or privacy settings.

2. Vocabulary Content, Game Progress, and Local Storage

VocabSpark may provide letter-based word puzzles, levels, and English vocabulary information, including words, definitions, parts of speech, synonyms, antonyms, and example sentences where available.

If the word database is stored locally in the App, viewing a word from that database does not require the search to be sent to our own servers. Game progress, preferences, and saved words may also be stored on your device.

Local data can generally be removed by using the App's available controls, clearing the App's storage, or uninstalling the App. Device backups or operating-system settings may affect how local data is retained.

3. Microphone, Voice, and Pronunciation

The App does not need to record your voice for its core word-gameplay. If a pronunciation feature uses your device's built-in text-to-speech service, it may speak a word or phrase aloud. Text-to-speech availability and processing depend on your device, installed voice data, and selected service. If a third-party speech service processes information, its own privacy policy applies.

4. Advertising

If ads are enabled, the free version of VocabSpark may show advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob.

Depending on the provider, your region, consent choices, and device settings, advertising services may process information such as:

  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers
  • Device, app, and approximate regional information
  • Ad views, clicks, and performance signals
  • Information used to deliver, measure, limit, or personalise advertising

Where required, consent choices or privacy options may be presented. You may also be able to manage ad personalisation or advertising identifiers through your device settings.

Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

5. In-App Purchases

If the App offers premium features or in-app purchases, transactions are handled by the relevant app store, such as Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card details through the App. The app store manages payment processing, billing, refunds, and subscription cancellation under its own terms and policies.

6. Third-Party Services

Depending on the version of the App, third-party services may be used for advertising, app-store billing, platform functionality, or diagnostics. These may include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob, if ads are enabled
  • Google Play Billing, if purchases are offered

Third-party providers handle information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control their independent data practices. Please review the policies of the services that are active in the App.

7. How Information Is Used

Information you send to us directly is used to respond to your message or support request. Technical information processed by third-party services may be used to provide their features, display and measure ads, process purchases, diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and maintain app reliability.

We do not sell personal information. VocabSpark does not use locally stored game progress, word searches, or favourites for our own server-side profiling.

8. Data Retention and Security

Data stored locally generally remains on your device until you remove it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Messages sent to our support email may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, keep appropriate records, or meet legal obligations.

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information under our control. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

VocabSpark: Word Game is an educational English vocabulary and word puzzle game. The App does not knowingly ask children to submit personal information directly to us. Third-party services, including advertising services, may have separate age-related requirements and data practices.

If the App is made available to children or is included in a children/families programme, advertising, consent, and data practices must be configured to comply with applicable laws and app-store policies. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us so we can review the request.

10. Your Choices

  • Use available in-app controls to remove saved words or reset progress, if supported.
  • Clear the App's storage or uninstall it to remove locally stored data, subject to device backups and operating-system behaviour.
  • Manage advertising identifiers and ad-personalisation settings through your device settings, where available.
  • Use any consent or privacy options shown by the App or its service providers.
  • Manage purchases or subscriptions through the relevant app store, if applicable.
  • You may choose not to contact us by email, although we then cannot respond to a support request.

11. International Data Processing

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Those providers are responsible for applying the safeguards and meeting the legal requirements that apply to their processing.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App, its services, or applicable requirements change. We will publish the revised policy with an updated effective date. Please review this page periodically.
